# Media Brief

## Primary angle
Australians' discretionary spending growth overtakes non-discretionary spending

## Headline options
1. Recreation spending leads Australian household spending growth
2. Discretionary spending growth overtakes non-discretionary spending
3. NT and WA lead the country for household spending growth

## Key statistics
- Recreation and culture: +9.7%
- Discretionary spending: +7.8%
- Non-discretionary spending: +5.6%
- Northern Territory: +10.6%
- Western Australia: +9.9%

## Suggested media verticals
- Consumer affairs
- Business
- Retail
- Lifestyle
- State-based news

## Methodology note
ABS Monthly Household Spending Indicator, July 2026.
Seasonally adjusted, current-price data.

## Important caveat
Higher spending can reflect higher prices, increased quantities purchased, or both.
